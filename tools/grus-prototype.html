GROOVEBOX · PROTOTYPE 0.6 · WORKING TITLE

Eight tracks, seven synth engines and a sampler, with a lock-based step sequencer, built as a prototype for an FM-1 firmware. The panel at the top controls everything the FM-1 will do, and the rest of the page shows the same thing in a larger format.

Encoders: drag up and down or scroll. Hold a white key and turn a KNOB to lock it on that step. On HOME, holding a step shows its own settings: velocity, pitch, chance, ratchet, ratchet curve, nudge and condition. On your computer keyboard, Q–I are steps 1–8 and A–K are steps 9–16, so you can hold a key and drag with the mouse at the same time. Shift-click a white key to keep it held until you click again. Hold FX for live effects, hold REC for scale and chords, hold SAVE to undo, hold GLO for setup. Press SAVE twice for the song.

    Mixer

    Also lockable per step. Lock Delay high on a single step for a dub throw.

    Samples

    Four slots of 7.4 s, the same as the FM-1's flash. Sound is kept at 22 kHz in 4-bit ADPCM, so you hear what the hardware will play. With slices, each key plays its own slice; with none, the keys play the sample in pitch. Click the waveform to hear a slice.

    All sound and timing is computed sample by sample, the same way the C code on the FM-1 will do it. Every engine has eight knobs, just like the FM-1. On the hardware the tracks are the black keys, the steps are the white keys and the lock types are a page you flip through.
